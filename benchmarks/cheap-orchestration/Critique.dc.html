The cheap-orchestration skill pushes the main loop (Opus 5.5) to hand work to Haiku or Sonnet subagents. It is made of separable pieces, which the builder above lets you switch on one by one.

The starting question: how many tokens and dollars does each model use with the full skill, with a lighter version, and with nothing at all? It became along the way: which piece does what, and which one costs without paying back?
